Tourist Places

Silicon Valley
Bengaluru

The vibrant capital city known for tech parks, Lalbagh Botanical Garden, and rich culture.

Coastal
Gokarna

Famous for pristine Om Beach, Kudle Beach, and the sacred Mahabaleshwar Temple.

Wildlife Reserve
Kabini & Nagarhole

A paradise for wildlife enthusiasts, famous for tigers, leopards, and black panthers.

Historical Places

UNESCO Site
Hampi

Capital of the Vijayanagara Empire with iconic ruins, Stone Chariot, and Virupaksha Temple.

Heritage Landmark
Mysuru Palace

The magnificent seat of the Wadiyar dynasty, grand illuminated palace during Dasara festival.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

Second steepest plunge waterfall in India created by Sharavathi river.

Coorg (Madikeri)

Known as the Scotland of India, famous for coffee plantations and mist hills.

Chikkamagaluru

Home to Mullayanagiri peak, lush tea estates, and scenic hiking trails.

Famous Foods

Mysore Masala Dosa

Crispy dosa with red chutney and potato palya.

Bisi Bele Bath

Spicy rice, lentils, and mixed vegetable delicacy.

Neer Dosa

Thin, soft rice crepes from Coastal Karnataka.

Mysore Pak

Melt-in-mouth sweet made with besan and ghee.

Travel Information

By Air

Kempegowda International Airport (Bengaluru), Mangaluru, and Hubballi Airports connect globally.

By Rail

Extensive South Western Railway network connects all major districts and heritage spots.

By Road

KSRTC bus services operate nationwide express and luxury buses to all destinations.
